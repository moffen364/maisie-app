# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Maisie — a single user running a personal PWA on her phone. She's in a task: checking today's plan, adding a quick item, reviewing the week, or glancing at finances. All use is personal and private; the app disappears into her routine.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered personal weekly planner. It helps Maisie plan her week on Sundays (with Claude), track what's on today, stay on top of todos, and see where her money goes — all in one lightweight PWA.

## Brand Personality

Soft, personal, delightful. Three words: gentle, yours, warm. The app should feel like a pretty notebook — something Maisie would actually want to look at, not a utility she has to use.

## Anti-references

- Corporate productivity apps (Linear, Notion dark, Jira) — too stark, too anonymous
- Fintech dashboards — no metric-hero cards, no data-heavy layouts
- Generic "AI tool" aesthetics — not a startup, not trying to impress anyone else

## Design Principles

1. **It's hers.** Every design decision should feel personal, not generic or corporate.
2. **Disappear into the task.** Interactions are fast and frictionless — the UI supports the habit, not the other way around.
3. **Simple, not sparse.** Clean enough that she can glance and go, but warm enough that it feels cared for.
4. **One thing at a time.** Cards, sheets, and steps are focused — no information overload.
5. **Consistent vocabulary.** Same button shapes, same card style, same section headers everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Single-user personal app; no formal WCAG target. Still: text must be readable without squinting, contrast must be comfortable for everyday use, touch targets must be comfortable (≥44px).
